Every brand sizes differently, so shoppers guess and pay for it in returns. Fit Passport learns your size from clothes that already fit you, and tells you what to buy at any store, with the reasons.

02How it works

1Your closet is the evidence

Add clothes that already fit. Each one teaches the engine how a brand runs on your body.

2One click on any product page

The Chrome extension reads the size chart in your browser. Only the product is sent, never the page.

Room to spareSnug

3Your size, and why

Every pick lists its evidence. A 3D body fitted to your numbers shows where it is tight or roomy.

03Why it is different, and where it stands

Honest by construction

  • Explained: every size lists the signals behind it
  • A chart it could not read is marked estimated
  • Private: precise measurements never leave your account
  • Learns from every keep, return and exchange you record

Built and live

  • Live at fitpassport.fit; extension on the Chrome Web Store
  • US, EU and UK sizes, and Chinese size codes
  • 103 garment types; 358 shop category names recognised
  • 3D body fitted to your girths within 1 cm in tests
