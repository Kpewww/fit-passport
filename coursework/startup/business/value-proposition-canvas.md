# Fit Passport — Value Proposition Canvas (v0.1)

Osterwalder VPC applied to the beachhead segment (US cross-brand shopper).

## Customer profile

### Jobs to be done
- Find something I like that will actually fit — without ordering multiple sizes.
- Trust a size recommendation enough to skip reading 20 reviews.
- Move to a new brand or region without relearning my size.
- Not feel silly for keeping detailed body measurements handy.

### Pains
- "Size M means five different things across five brands."
- "I've returned this brand three times and I still don't know my size."
- "The retailer says wear-a-size-up, but based on which reference?"
- "I don't want to enter 12 body measurements just to buy a T-shirt."
- Losing time, losing shipping fees, feeling wasteful about returns.

### Gains
- Buy once, keep it, move on.
- Feel *confident*, not "I hope this works out."
- Have a portable record of what worked, even for gifts.
- Extend to shoes / pets / rings later without changing tools.

---

## Value map

### Products & services
- The Fit Passport (portable profile).
- The Closet (known-good anchors).
- Check-a-product (paste URL → ranked sizes with reasons).
- History (keep/return/exchange as ground truth).
- Household & pet extensions (roadmap).

### Pain relievers
- **Cross-brand normalization** — the passport knows US alpha ↔ EU numeric mappings; you don't have to.
- **Explanation** — every recommendation says *why*, so you can trust it or adjust.
- **Low-friction onboarding** — every measurement is optional; 3 known-good items is enough.
- **Confidence signal** — we tell you when we're guessing.
- **Data control** — your profile is yours; export/delete visible.

### Gain creators
- **Learning loop** — every recorded outcome sharpens tomorrow's recommendation.
- **Any-store workflow** — no retailer integration required; paste any URL.
- **Cross-domain** — same profile pattern extends to shoes, pets, rings.
- **Explainability compounds trust** — users understand what fit means for their body.

## The one-sentence VP
> **For cross-brand online shoppers who lose confidence at the size dropdown,**
> **Fit Passport is a portable fit identity** that recommends the right size
> at any store with a reason you can trust, **unlike retailer-embedded sizing**
> **tools that reset every time you switch brands.**
