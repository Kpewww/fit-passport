# Startup course track

This track treats Fit Passport as a **complete product and venture**, not just a technical system.

Focus:
- customer discovery and validation
- target segment and unmet needs
- competitive landscape and positioning
- value proposition and business model
- pricing / monetization hypotheses
- go-to-market and messaging
- product roadmap and end-to-end delivery
- brand, community/retention hypotheses and pitch materials

## Structure

- [business/](business/) — BMC, VPC, customer interview guide, project plan, messaging, risk/legal and weekly review
- [proposals/](proposals/) — proposal history and current startup proposal
- [prospectus/](prospectus/) — founder/prospectus-level product and venture material
- [product-design/](product-design/) — brand/product-facing design material, information architecture and community/retention concepts

## Shared implementation

The startup course does not get its own copy of the app. The live product remains in `app-web/`, and brand build assets remain in `brand/`.

Technical feasibility documents stay with the shared engineering documentation under `docs/design/` and `docs/memory/`; this folder contains the market/product lens.
